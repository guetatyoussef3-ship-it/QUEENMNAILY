Payments

The price depends on the type of nails you choose and the service provided, whether the appointment is at my place or if I travel to the client’s location. Currently accepted payment method: Cash only. Please make the payment in advance to confirm your appointment.

* Cancellations & Rescheduling

Please reschedule or cancel your appointment at least 48 hours before your scheduled time. If this is not done, the appointment will be considered cancelled and 50% of the service price will be charged. If an emergency happens, please contact me by message so we can find a solution.

Appointment Rules !

Please arrive at your scheduled time.There is a 10-minute grace period only. After 10 minutes, your appointment may be considered cancelled as a “no show”.

Important Rules

- Please do not arrive with old nail extensions, glue, or products that need removal before the appointment. - Pregnant clients are not accepted for services for safety reasons. - Please inform me in advance about any special nail conditions.
